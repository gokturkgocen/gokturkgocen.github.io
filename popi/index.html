Tap · Pop · Paint

Tap a bubble and every touching bubble of the same colour pops with it. Big groups leave star bombs and rainbows behind, every pop splashes paint, and when the level is done the painting you made is revealed.

Help

  • Lost progress? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Ads and tracking. Change tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and consent choices in the game's Settings › Ad preferences (where available).
  • No sound? The game follows the ring/silent switch. Turn silent mode off, and check the sound and music buttons on the title screen.
